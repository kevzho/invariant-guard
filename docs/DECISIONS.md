| I-025 | (Audit A-02) Finite interval bounds are clamped at magnitude 2^256 by widening (lo > L becomes L, lo < -L becomes -inf, and symmetrically for hi). | I-026 | (Audit A-03) A join of a variable that is unbound on some incoming path produces a PHI marked `maybe_unbound` (transitively through earlier such PHIs).
